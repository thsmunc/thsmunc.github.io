<h2><span style="color: #550000;">About THSMUNC</span></h2>
<p style="padding-left: 30px;">THSMUNC was founded in 2020 by a group of six seniors from the Law & Public Policy magnet program at Towson High School, THSMUNC I took place on April 17, 2021. Our mission is to create a welcoming and professional conference that will foster formative debate and help all who attend grow as speakers, diplomats, and leaders. We strive to provide the highest-quality Model UN experience for our delegates, and to help them to form meaningful memories that will last for years to come.</p>
